Green Manufacturing Reconstruction

The current value of all factory machinery is about $1.5 trillion. Therefore, to replace most machinery, including research and development, could reasonably done for $2 trillion over 20 years, or $100 billion per year. The machinery would not emit greenhouse gas emissions, would not pollute, and would create products that could be recycled/reused.
